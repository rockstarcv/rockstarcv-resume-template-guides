What Acamar is designed for

Acamar is a polished, one-page, two-column resume template for experienced professionals who want their leadership, results, and career story to scan clearly. Its wide main column gives profile and experience content room; the narrower side column holds supporting details. The visual style uses charcoal typography and a restrained bronze accent.

The sample layout includes a name and role header, a profile, professional experience, contact details, education, skills, languages, and references. Replace every sample name, employer, date, contact detail, and achievement with your own accurate information. Keep only sections that are useful for the role.

Acamar includes a portrait frame. Whether to use a photograph depends on your country, industry, employer, and application instructions. You can replace the image or remove the frame if a photo is not appropriate for your target market.

Acamar is composed for a single portrait page. Start with the supplied page geometry and edit the existing objects before adding new ones. Add a second page only when you have substantial, relevant evidence that cannot fit clearly on one page.

Start your resume

  1. Open the Acamar product page and choose the builder action available to your account.
  2. Sign in to the RockStarCV account that has access to Acamar.
  3. When the resume opens, give it a clear working title in the top bar, such as “Operations Director — Acme.” The title helps you identify the saved resume; it is not a printed resume heading.
  4. Replace the sample name, headline, contact details, profile, and work history. Do not leave example information in the final document.
  5. Save as you work. The editor marks changes as unsaved until you save them.
  6. Use View / PDF to inspect the export before sending it with an application.

Recommended order: write and edit the content first, then make small visual adjustments. This keeps the layout focused on the evidence in your resume.

Understand and write each section

Name and professional headline

Use your name as the most prominent identity. The headline should describe the type of work you do or the role you are targeting. Keep it specific and consistent with your experience.

Contact details

Keep the details a recruiter needs to reach you: a professional email address, phone number, location, and relevant portfolio or LinkedIn link. Use a complete, working URL. Remove a street address or other detail when it is unnecessary for the role or local hiring practice.

Profile

Write a short introduction that connects your experience to the role. Lead with your scope, specialty, or business context, then name the value you bring. Avoid generic claims that are not supported elsewhere in the resume.

Professional experience

For each role, make the title, employer, location, and dates easy to identify. Use concise bullets to show decisions, actions, and outcomes. When you include figures, use only numbers you can explain and defend. Put the most relevant evidence first.

For an experienced candidate, useful context can include team size, budget, portfolio, territory, customer group, or operational scale. Choose only the details that matter to the target role.

Education

List relevant qualifications, institutions, locations, and dates where useful. Give the most relevant or recent qualification the clearest position. Remove sample qualifications that are not yours.

Skills

Keep skills relevant to the role and support important skills with examples in your experience section. The five-dot indicators are visual summaries; they are not proof of proficiency. Do not rely on a rating to replace evidence.

Languages

Name each language and use an accurate, clear proficiency description. Keep the wording consistent with what you can demonstrate in work.

References

Include references only when they are useful and you have permission to share the people’s details. Otherwise, remove or replace the sample reference area with relevant evidence. Never leave fictional sample names or contact information in your resume.

Find your way around the builder

The editor has a top bar, a canvas in the center, navigation panels on the left, and object properties on the right. Select an object on the canvas to see its available properties. You can hide or reopen the side panels if you need more canvas space.

Top bar

  • Resume title: rename the saved working document.
  • Zoom: choose 100%, 75%, or 50%, or use Fit and Fit width to size the page to the available canvas.
  • Undo / Redo: step backward or forward through recent unsaved changes.
  • Save status: check whether the current resume is saved or still has unsaved changes.
  • View / PDF: inspect the rendered output and the pages you are about to export.
  • Save: save the resume changes to your account.

Left-side panels

  • Elements: add text, an image, a shape, or a Lucide icon.
  • Colors: edit document color swatches or apply a readable preset palette. Reset colors to return to the template defaults.
  • Pages: select a page, add a page, or delete an extra page. The last remaining page cannot be deleted.
  • Templates: search for a template, filter by column layout, and apply a different starting layout. Review the entire resume after switching because the object arrangement can change.
  • Designer: use Tidy up design to make a supported layout cleanup. Review the result and use Undo before saving if it does not suit your resume.
  • My Profile: maintain reusable profile details and apply them to a resume.
  • Settings: change paper size, orientation, page color, or reset the applied template layout.
  • Resources: open builder guidance and RockStarCV career resources.

Canvas and floating controls

The canvas represents the actual resume page. Click an object to select it. Drag to move it; use the resize handles to change its size. Double-click a text object to edit its words directly. Arrow keys can make small position adjustments. Use the floating toolbar or Properties panel for duplication, locking, grouping, layering, and deletion. Locked objects need to be unlocked before you can change them.

Edit text and typography

  1. Select the text frame you want to change.
  2. Double-click the text on the canvas and replace the sample wording, or press Enter when the text object is selected.
  3. Use the Properties panel to adjust the full text object’s font, size, weight, line height, letter spacing, alignment, regular or italic style, underline, text color, and shadow.
  4. Use the font picker to search available fonts. If the editor offers an add-font action for your account, follow its prompt to add a font asset.
  5. Zoom in for careful editing, then return to Fit to check the full page.

Text-frame typography applies to the whole object. If you only need to change a phrase inside it, select the words on the canvas and use the inline text controls available there. After changing font size or line spacing, check for clipped text and crowded sections.

Add, replace, and crop an image

Replace Acamar’s portrait

  1. Select the portrait frame.
  2. In Properties, choose Replace image. Select an image from the Media Library.
  3. Choose a fit: Cover fills the frame and crops the image; Contain shows the whole image and may leave unused space.
  4. Choose a mask such as rectangle, square, circle, ellipse, rounded, triangle, diamond, or hexagon.
  5. If you use Cover, adjust the horizontal and vertical focal point to keep the important part of the portrait in frame.
  6. Add concise alt text that describes the image. Check the result at both page-fit and close-up zoom.

Add another image

Open Elements and choose Image, then select an asset from the Media Library. Use the object’s Properties to change its mask, fit, crop focal point, alt text, dimensions, or aspect-ratio lock. Keep decorative images secondary to the resume evidence.

Profile-photo uploads in My Profile are separate from images added to a resume. The profile photo accepts JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP files up to 1 MB and is stored as WebP. A photo-capable template can use it when you apply that profile.

Edit shapes, lines, and icons

Open Elements to add a shape or icon. Select it to edit its properties.

  • Shapes: choose a rectangle, ellipse, or line. Adjust dimensions and aspect-ratio lock. A shape can use a solid fill or linear gradient, a stroke color and width, a solid, dashed, or dotted stroke, a corner radius for rectangles, and a subtle shadow.
  • Lines: choose solid, dashed, dotted, double, wave, or zigzag. Depending on the pattern, adjust thickness, line ends, spacing, length, or wave/zigzag size.
  • Icons: choose from the Lucide icon library, then adjust dimensions, color, stroke weight, and shadow.

Use lines and icons only when they improve navigation or hierarchy. Acamar’s bronze rules and restrained two-column structure are part of its visual rhythm; large decorative additions can make the page feel crowded.

Change colors and page settings

Colors

In Colors, edit a document swatch to update matching uses throughout the resume, or apply one of the readable palette presets. The original Acamar accent is bronze. Use strong contrast for text and check the PDF at normal viewing size. Choose Reset colors to restore the template’s default colors.

Paper, orientation, and background

In Settings, choose A4 or US Letter. Acamar is designed for portrait orientation; switching to landscape or a different paper size changes the available space, so review every object and page break afterward. Page color can be set for the active page or applied across all pages. Use Reset applied layout if extensive manual changes have made the original geometry difficult to recover.

Pages

Use the page tabs to choose the page you are editing. Add a page only when it gives meaningful content enough room. Delete an extra page after moving or consolidating any useful information. Preview the full document after adding or removing pages.

Move, group, and arrange objects

  • Move: drag an unlocked object on the canvas or nudge it with the arrow keys.
  • Resize: use the edge or corner handles, or enter exact dimensions in Properties. Lock the aspect ratio when a photo or icon must keep its proportions.
  • Duplicate / delete: use the floating toolbar or Properties controls. Confirm the selected item before deleting it.
  • Layer: bring an object forward or send it backward when objects overlap.
  • Lock / unlock: lock a finished object to avoid moving it while editing nearby content.
  • Group / ungroup: select multiple objects and group them to move or resize them together. Ungroup when you need to edit one item independently.
  • Align / distribute: with multiple selected objects, align their edges or centers. Select three or more objects to distribute equal gaps horizontally or vertically.

Make one change at a time, then check both the selected area and the full page. A small movement to a divider can affect the balance between Acamar’s columns.

Use My Profile

My Profile stores reusable career details separately from a resume. Changes to the saved profile do not silently change resumes to which it was previously applied.

  1. Create a profile and give it a name you will recognize.
  2. Add your identity details, professional headline, location, contact methods, website and LinkedIn URL, industry, pronouns if you choose, and a concise summary.
  3. Add reusable section entries, one per line.
  4. To import information, choose a LinkedIn PDF or Word file, a text file, or paste profile text. File import requires selectable text and accepts files up to 8 MB and 30 PDF pages.
  5. Review every imported field for accuracy and formatting.
  6. Choose Save profile. Then choose Apply to this resume when you are ready to copy the profile details into the open resume.

Applying a profile is a starting point, not a final review. Check the resulting text and layout in Acamar, and save the resume separately from the profile.

Extensive resume writing tips for a Sales Director

Acamar’s senior two-column layout works best when commercial scope and measurable outcomes lead. Use these template-specific prompts to shape the headline, profile, experience, and supporting column around the Sales Director role. Keep every claim accurate, attributable, and ready to explain.

Acamar works best when executive confidence is matched by precise, credible evidence. Use these prompts to turn responsibilities into a clear business story.

Open with commercial value

Replace a generic summary with the market, customer, revenue, transformation, or leadership problem you are trusted to solve.

Lead each role with scope

State team size, portfolio value, territory, budget, client segment, or operational scale before listing achievements.

Make results measurable

Use truthful numbers: revenue gained, margin protected, retention improved, cycle time reduced, or risk removed.

Use the left column strategically

Keep contact details lean. Prioritise relevant education, languages, and skills that reinforce the target role.

Replace ratings with evidence

Five-dot skill indicators are visual summaries, not proof. Back important skills with examples inside experience bullets.

Write for people and ATS

Use standard role terms naturally, keep text selectable, and avoid forcing every keyword into the document.

A stronger Acamar bullet

Instead of “Responsible for account growth,” write “Expanded a $24M enterprise portfolio by 18% while improving renewal rate from 82% to 91%.” Use only figures you can defend in an interview.

Write each Acamar section for the target role

  • Headline and profile: name the commercial remit, market, customer segment, or transformation work you lead. Make the role match clear without relying on broad labels such as “results-driven.”
  • Experience: start with the size of the team, territory, portfolio, budget, or revenue responsibility. Then explain the decision or action and the measurable change in revenue, margin, retention, cycle time, or risk.
  • Skills: select a short set of role-relevant strengths and support them with examples in the experience section. Treat visual rating dots as decoration, not evidence.
  • Education and supporting details: keep relevant qualifications and languages in the compact column; remove details that do not help this application.
  • Proof: check dates, figures, currency, team scope, and ownership. Distinguish your contribution from the team’s result and prepare the source or calculation behind each metric.

Bullet pattern: “Led [scope or commercial challenge] by [specific decision or action], improving [verified outcome] from [baseline] to [result] over [timeframe].” Use only facts you can substantiate.

Save and export a PDF

  1. Check that the top-bar status says Saved. If it says Unsaved, choose Save and wait for the saved status.
  2. Choose View / PDF to preview the output, not just the editor canvas.
  3. Review every page for clipped text, awkward line breaks, missing images, inconsistent spacing, and incorrect contact details.
  4. Confirm the page size and orientation are appropriate for the country and application.
  5. Make sure your name, employers, job titles, dates, section headings, and body text are selectable text in the PDF. Do not submit a screenshot of the resume.
  6. Check that the document reads in a sensible order: identity and contact details, profile, experience, then supporting sections.
  7. Return to the editor, correct any issues, save again, and preview the final PDF once more.

Keeping the text selectable and the section names familiar can help people and software read your resume, but no template can guarantee how every employer’s application system will process a file.

Final application check

  • Every sample name and placeholder has been replaced or removed.
  • Every achievement and number is accurate and explainable.
  • The content matches the specific role and puts the strongest evidence first.
  • The portrait choice fits the target market and application instructions.
  • The resume is readable at normal size and not crowded with extra shapes or icons.
  • The saved PDF has the correct page size, no clipping, and selectable text in a sensible reading order.
  • The final resume is saved in the correct account and has a clear working title.
